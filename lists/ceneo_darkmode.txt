
! Ceneo dark mode for uBlock Origin (ceneo.pl)
!
! Design
!  Ceneo paints most of its UI through design-system color tokens
!  (--ceneo-color-*, defined on :root): surfaces, text, borders, accents.
!  Re-defining the semantic tokens re-colors the bulk of the page in one place,
!  before paint, so there is no flash and nothing reverts (same approach as the
!  OLX list; no :matches-css()). A number of containers hard-code #fff / #f2f5f7
!  instead of tokens, so they get targeted :style() hooks in section 2.
! Palette: deep navy surfaces (#16202a / #1a2530 / #232f3b, matching Ceneo's own
! --ceneo-color-background-black), light text, Ceneo orange accent kept (#ff6400).
!
! IMAGES: product photos must stay untouched. We only recolor placeholder/wrapper
! backgrounds, never <img>/<picture>/<video>; images are force-kept transparent
! and unfiltered in section 3 so photos look exactly as on the light theme.

! ==== 1. Theme tokens (ceneo design system) ====
! Surfaces -> navy; text -> near-white; borders -> dark; keep orange/blue accents.
