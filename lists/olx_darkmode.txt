! OLX dark mode for uBlock Origin (olx.pl)
!
! Design
!  OLX paints almost everything through CSS custom properties (design tokens,
!  --colors* / --color-*, defined on :root). Re-defining the tokens re-colors
!  text, surfaces, borders, buttons, inputs, badges, dropdowns and hover states
!  in one place, instantly and regardless of the (hashed) class names.
!  This is why there is NO :matches-css() here: that engine runs only after a DOM
!  mutation and with a delay, which caused the flashing on load and the white
!  flash when a card (e.g. ad-price-container) was re-mounted during scroll.
!  Token overrides apply before paint, so there is no flash and nothing reverts.
!  A few leftovers (hard-coded colors, SVG icons) are handled with stable hooks.
! Palette follows OLX: charcoal surfaces, teal accent #3CDED8, near-white text.

! ==== 1. Theme tokens (nexus design system) ====

! (invisible or inverted on dark), so they are remapped to light translucent white.
